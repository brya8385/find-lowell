# Find Lowell: audit, 24 Sep 2026

Audit of https://brya8385.github.io/find-lowell/ as published on 11 Sep 2026 (`data/doors.json`,
`asof: 2026-09-11`, 658 entries). Read-only: nothing in `index.html`, `app.v4.js` or
`data/doors.json` was changed. The audit checked the page load, current counts against the sources,
15 sampled doors, classifier quality and the four data rules.

## Summary

- **The page loads.** Page, `app.v4.js` and `doors.json` return HTTP 200 with no console errors.
  `doors.json` sends `access-control-allow-origin: *`. The page renders at desktop width and at 375 px
  with no horizontal scroll. ZIP 11231 returns 179 stores within 50 miles.
- **The data is 13 days old.** `doors.json` was last modified Fri 11 Sep 23:01 GMT and nothing has
  updated it since. Since then 29 Tier A stores have dropped off the menu feed and 16 have joined;
  44 have gone out of stock and 13 have come back.
- **14 defects: 5 high, 6 medium, 3 low.** The three worst:
  1. **239 of 534 Tier A menu buttons (45%) go to a 404.** 232 carry malformed links such as
     `:///Smokes`, which resolve to a GitHub Pages "File not found". 7 carry the bare
     `https://dutchie.com/stores`, which is a Dutchie 404.
  2. **16 duplicate entries.** 8 NY stores appear twice in Tier A. 7 Tier B entries duplicate a Tier A
     store, the case the Tier B dedupe rule was meant to stop. 1 NM entry appears twice.
  3. **6 pins are 86–884 miles from the store.** Two CO pins are in Illinois and Montana, two
     Albuquerque stores are pinned near Farmington and the Texas border, a San Jose store is pinned in
     the San Diego area, and one IL store carries another store's address.
- **Rules:** the price-leak check passes (0 Tier B/C doors carry a price). There are no $0.01 samples.
  "Shake" is normalised. All Terrain = Variety holds on 43 of 45 rows.

## 6. Defects

"Rebuild fixes it" means Part 2's daily rebuild from the current sources fixes it, given the
condition stated. "Own fix" means it needs work beyond the rebuild.

### High

**D1. 239 of 534 Tier A menu buttons go to a 404.**
- 232 hrefs are malformed: `:///Smokes` ×142, `:///Quicks` ×60, `:///Outlaws` ×29, plus one
  Google-redirect URL.
  - The browser resolves them to `https://brya8385.github.io/find-lowell/:///Smokes`, a GitHub Pages
    "404 File not found" (opened).
  - By state: NY 110, NJ 84, IL 38.
- 7 are the bare `https://dutchie.com/stores`, which is a Dutchie 404 (opened): OH 4, NY 1, NJ 1, MO 1.
- Cause: the "menu root = host + first path segment" rule. It cuts
  `dutchie.com/stores/<slug>/…` to `dutchie.com/stores` and broke on non-absolute stored URLs.
- *Rebuild fixes it*, with two conditions:
  - Take the URL from the feed. Today it gives an absolute `recreationalURL` for 512 of 514 listing
    doors.
  - Keep the slug for `dutchie.com/{embedded-menu,stores,dispensary}/<slug>`. Add a gate that every
    `u` is an absolute http(s) URL.

**D2. The stock and store lists are 13 days old.**
- 29 stores dropped off the feed and 16 joined. 44 went out of stock and 13 came back.
- Spot-check: Alta and SWAY show "in stock" with no Lowell on the live menu.
- ZIP 11231's first result (Erudito) lists no Lowell today.
- *Rebuild fixes it* (daily).

**D3. 16 duplicate entries.**
- NY Tier A, 8 stores listed twice: Beleaf Brooklyn, Budd Barn / Canna Planet Herkimer, Catskill
  Mountain High ("Mountian"), Gaslight Forest Ave, Herbal Woodstock, New Amsterdam NYC, Puro Vita.
  Toke Cannabis Westchester is listed at both 471 and 571 McLean Ave; the feed has 471.
  - Cause: the vendor carries two retailer ids for one store, and the build dedupes Tier B against A
    but never A against A.
- Tier B entries that duplicate a Tier A store, 7:
  - NJ: Mindlift Plainfield, NJ Leaf Freehold, NJ Leaf Keansburg, Rush Budz, The Station Hoboken,
    The Botanist Collingswood.
  - IL: Bloom Wellness Hometown.
  - Cause: the street key missed Avenue/Ave, Street/St., "New Jersey 36"/"NJ-36" and Southwest/SW,
    plus a wrong ZIP in our retailer record (08310 for 08103).
- NM Tier C: "Everest — Sunland Park" appears twice at one pin. The report has two Sunland Park
  codes (EDW02, EV10), so it may be two stores under one label.
- *Rebuild fixes it* if it dedupes A↔A as well as B↔A, with an abbreviation table and a
  number + street match within the city. Toke (471/571) needs a one-line override.

**D4. 6 pins are far from the store.**
- CO "Everyday Wellness — 6th Ave, Aurora" is at 41.743, −88.293: Aurora, **Illinois**, 884 mi
  from Denver.
- CO "Emerald Fields — South Boulder" is at 45.607, −112.061: Boulder, **Montana**, 541 mi from
  Denver.
- NM "R.Greenleaf — Cottonwood" is near Farmington, 150.8 mi from the store at 10250 Cottonwood
  Park NW, Albuquerque (chain's store page).
- NM "R.Greenleaf — West Side" is near the Texas line, 219.1 mi from 5201 Ouray Rd NW,
  Albuquerque.
- CA "Harborside" is in the San Diego area. The account is San Jose Wellness DBA Harborside, 419 mi
  away.
- IL "Snap Canna - Pontiac" sits at Snap Canna Elk Grove's address (840 E Higgins Rd). The vendor
  directory gives both ids that address; Pontiac is 86 mi away.
- *Own fix.* The CO/NM/CA pins came from geocoding neighbourhood or city labels, and the Pontiac
  address is wrong at the source. A rebuild reproduces all six.
  - Needed: address overrides; street addresses for the Vireo banners from their store pages
    (R.Greenleaf lists all 13); and a gate that rejects coordinates outside the state. That gate
    catches only the 2 CO pins.

**D5. Tier B says "Carries Lowell" at stores whose live menu lists no Lowell.**
- 21 of the 27 unique IL Tier B stores have a menu the feed reads today with no Lowell on it.
  Examples: Consume Cannabis ×6, Curaleaf ×4, Thrive ×4, and Kush 21 (spot-check #11).
- The spec defines Tier B as stores no menu feed covers.
- Cause, likely: "not in the Lowell snapshot rows" was used as the test. For IL the snapshot table
  holds Lowell rows only, so a store the feed reads without Lowell looks the same as one it never
  reads.
- Their status on Sep 11 can't be recovered.
- *Rebuild fixes it* if it tests "does the feed read this store at all" per retailer
  (`/retailers/{id}/products`, any brand). Stores read with no Lowell stay off the map.
